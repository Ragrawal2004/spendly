Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and account password, along with the expense records you log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information you provide to operate the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns of your spending. We may also use aggregated, anonymized data to understand usage patterns and improve Spendly's features. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting, and these providers only process data as necessary to deliver the Service, under their own confidentiality and security obligations.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request access to or deletion of your data, please reach out to us through the contact details provided in your account settings or on our support page.
